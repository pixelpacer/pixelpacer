# Kevin Nicholas

**Principal UX Designer. I make AI a dependable part of how product teams design and build.**

For over a decade I designed enterprise learning and talent products at Skillsoft, including Percipio, the skills platform that serves 42 million employees at more than 3,000 organizations. Lately I've focused on the hardest part of AI-assisted product work: getting from a big idea to work that fits the design system, holds up for users, and gives engineers something they can actually build from.

I started as a web designer who built what I designed. AI brought that back, and I turned it into a system a whole product org could use.

## Selected work

- **Introduced a three-stage path for AI prototypes** (conceptual, experience, dev-ready) that teams across the product org adopted. Each stage gets the right depth of design system, so fidelity and tokens go where they pay off.
- **Conceived and championed UX Lab Portal**, a secure home for AI-built prototypes. Built with DevOps, it's used daily in customer demos.
- **Built a Claude Code handoff skill** that turns one sprint's slice of a prototype into a standalone HTML spec for engineering and a visual reference for QA.
- **Led the learner experience for CAISY**, Skillsoft's award-winning AI conversation simulator, taking it from an engineering proof of concept to a full experience. Usage grew 146% year over year.
- **Created Skill Benchmarks** from scratch. Completions grew 161% year over year.
- **Designed the digital badge system** behind 45M+ badges issued from 2021 to 2023 alone.

## How I build

- **Tokens first.** Color, type, and spacing live in one place, so every screen pulls from the same source.
- **Accessible by default.** WCAG 2.2 AA, full keyboard support, and an axe check before anything is called done.
- **AI with guardrails.** Each repo has a `CLAUDE.md` that spells out the standards the AI builds to: tokens, accessibility, naming, and copy rules.
